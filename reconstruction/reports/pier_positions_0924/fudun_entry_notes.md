# Fudun east entry / P248 area comparison

The official ground/B1 diagram shows the eastern entrance descending eastwards. Street View shows the entry branching from the south carriageway next to the visible P248 and the following splitter-island shaft. The original COMP_ACCESS_復敦_2 instead descended westwards and explicitly carried an unverified-direction property.

A reversible XY comparison rotates the original ramp and its two walls to an estimated -7.5-degree eastward alignment. Original Z profile, 3.5m width, approximately40.2m plan length and B1 depth remain unchanged assumptions. The working top center is estimated10m west and8m north of the observed splitter shaft, not a surveyed gate point. A4.1m-wide estimated at-grade feeder is cut through the median following the photographed branch. Existing road/median masks are repaired at the old ramp and cut at the comparison ramp. Original geometry is retained outside the working scene.

P248 south/north are provisionally associated with old model734/735. The next same-span pair east of P248 is provisionally associated with736/737; its own number has not been read. Each new position has two directly centered December2024 Street View bearings; no independent third-view check yet. P248 north/south spacing is approximately12.05m. No height, cap, bearing or deck corrections are applied.

Validation: comparison road/median meshes have no nonmanifold edges or zero-area faces after1mm degenerate-edge cleanup. Four adjacent piers are inside the estimated median, outside the ramp opening.
